Growing up with—or even marrying into—a family that includes a narcissistic mother can be a profoundly challenging and disorienting experience. The saying, “If Momma ain’t happy, nobody’s gonna be happy,” holds especially true when dealing with a narcissistic mother or mother-in-law. If you’ve grown up with a narcissistic mother, you may not know any different.
